# Data Privacy and Retention

> **Sample policy for PolicyLens testing.** This is general example content,
> not an approved company policy or legal advice. Data handling must be
> reviewed against applicable privacy laws and organization requirements.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This sample explains how personnel should handle personal, confidential, and
business information during collection, use, storage, sharing, and deletion.
It applies to information in paper records, email, applications, devices, and
other systems.

## 2. Collect and use only what is needed

Collect information for a defined, legitimate work purpose. Before collecting
or reusing personal information, use an approved process and provide required
notices. Do not request sensitive details merely because they might be useful
later. Use information only for approved purposes and limit access to people
who need it for their work.

## 3. Protect and share information

Use approved storage, access controls, and secure sharing methods. Check the
recipient and permissions before sending a file or granting access. Do not
put personal or confidential information in public links, personal accounts,
unapproved tools, or prompts to external services unless the organization has
approved that use.

Share information with vendors only after the required review and agreement
are in place. Use the minimum information necessary, and follow any geographic
or access restrictions set by the organization.

## 4. Accuracy and individual requests

Keep personal information reasonably accurate for its approved purpose.
Privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information
should be forwarded to the privacy contact rather than answered informally.
The privacy team determines the appropriate response and any applicable
exceptions.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Keep records only for the approved retention period or as long as needed for
their documented business purpose, subject to legal, regulatory, audit, and
litigation requirements. Follow the organization's retention schedule.
Securely delete or destroy information when it is no longer required. Do not
delete records that are subject to a preservation hold or investigation.

## 6. Suspected privacy incident

Promptly report lost devices, misdirected messages, unauthorized access,
unexpected disclosure, or other suspected exposure through the incident
reporting channel. Include what information may be involved, when it
happened, and steps already taken. Do not conceal the incident or independently
contact affected people unless instructed by the response team.

## 7. Responsibilities

Personnel must complete required training and follow approved procedures.
Managers should ensure access is appropriate for each role. The privacy and
security teams maintain detailed procedures, review risks, and coordinate
responses to data incidents.
