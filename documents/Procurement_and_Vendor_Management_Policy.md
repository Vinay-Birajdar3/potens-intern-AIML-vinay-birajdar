# Procurement and Vendor Management

> **Sample policy for PolicyLens testing.** This is general example content,
> not an approved purchasing rule. Replace it with organization-approved
> approval levels, financial controls, and local requirements.

## 1. Purpose

Purchases and vendor relationships should support a legitimate business need,
provide reasonable value, and protect the organization's information,
finances, and reputation. Personnel must follow the current approval matrix
and use approved purchasing channels.

## 2. Before selecting a vendor

The requesting team should describe the business need, expected deliverables,
budget source, and selection criteria. Compare suitable options using
consistent, role-relevant criteria when practical. Do not split purchases or
change descriptions to avoid an approval limit.

## 3. Vendor review

Before sharing data or signing a commitment, complete reviews appropriate to
the service. These may include security, privacy, legal, financial, insurance,
accessibility, and operational-risk checks. Vendors that will access company
systems or personal information require the relevant specialist review and
approved contract terms.

## 4. Approvals and commitments

Obtain documented approval from authorized budget owners and control
functions before placing an order, signing a contract, or promising payment.
Only designated signatories may bind the organization. Purchase orders,
contracts, and invoices should describe the same approved goods or services.

## 5. Conflicts, gifts, and fair dealing

Disclose personal or financial relationships with a vendor before taking part
in its selection or oversight. Do not request or accept gifts, favors, or
hospitality that could influence—or appear to influence—a business decision.
Follow the organization's code of conduct and gift-approval process.

## 6. Contract and vendor oversight

The business owner should monitor deliverables, service quality, access, and
renewal dates. Changes in scope, price, data use, or access require review
and approval before they take effect. Remove vendor access when it is no
longer needed and follow contract and records-retention requirements when
the relationship ends.

## 7. Invoices and records

Verify that invoices match approved orders and accepted work before payment.
Report duplicate, inaccurate, suspicious, or unexpected charges to Finance.
Keep selection notes, approvals, contracts, and receipts in the approved
records location so decisions can be reviewed.
