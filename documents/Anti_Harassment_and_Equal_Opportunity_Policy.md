# Respect at Work and Equal Opportunity

> **Sample policy for PolicyLens testing.** This is general example content,
> not an approved company policy or legal advice. Local law and organization
> procedures may provide additional rights and requirements.

## 1. Commitment

The organization aims to provide a workplace where people are treated with
dignity and have fair access to employment opportunities. Employment decisions
should be based on role-related skills, qualifications, performance, and
business needs, consistent with applicable law.

## 2. Harassment and discrimination

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, and unwanted conduct that
creates an intimidating or hostile work environment are not acceptable.
Conduct may be verbal, written, physical, visual, or online, and may occur
in person, remotely, during work travel, or at a work-related event.

Examples may include slurs, repeated degrading comments, unwanted advances,
sharing offensive material, deliberate exclusion, or treating someone
unfairly because of a legally protected characteristic. This list is
illustrative, not exhaustive.

## 3. Fair employment practices

Recruiting, hiring, pay, assignments, development, promotion, performance
management, and separation should follow fair, job-related criteria.
Reasonable accommodation requests should be directed to Human Resources or
the responsible accommodation contact for an individualized review under
applicable law and company procedures.

## 4. Reporting a concern

Anyone who experiences or witnesses concerning conduct may speak with a
manager, Human Resources, or an available reporting channel. A report should
include relevant details when possible, such as what happened, when and where
it occurred, who was involved, and any witnesses or records.

## 5. Review and privacy

The organization should review concerns promptly and impartially, speak with
relevant people, consider available information, and document appropriate
next steps. Information should be shared only as needed for a fair review or
as required by law. Absolute confidentiality may not be possible.

## 6. No retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, requests an
accommodation, or participates honestly in a review is prohibited. Concerns
about retaliation should be reported through the same channels.

## 7. Responsibilities and support

Managers should take concerns seriously, avoid promising a particular
outcome, and promptly refer reports to the designated team. Employees should
cooperate honestly with reviews and treat everyone respectfully. Anyone in
immediate danger should contact local emergency services.
